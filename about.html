⭐ 01. Our Story

From a Living Room

It started with a simple passion for Catan and a crowded apartment table. We realized that in a digital world, people were craving real, face-to-face connection.

"What began as a dream to build a 'home away from home' became a reality in 2024. Today, we are proud to be the heart of the local gaming community."
